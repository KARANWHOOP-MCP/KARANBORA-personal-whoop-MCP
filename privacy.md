Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the personal WHOOP MCP integration developed by Karan Bora accesses and handles information when connecting WHOOP data to compatible applications through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

The integration is intended for personal use. This policy explains the intended data-handling practices of the integration.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user and the available WHOOP API access, the integration may access personal fitness and wellness data, including:

* Sleep and recovery information.
* Heart rate and heart rate variability.
* Workout and activity data.
* Strain and other available health metrics.
* Basic account information made available by WHOOP.

The integration only accesses data made available through the authorized WHOOP API and granted permissions.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the integration is used to provide fitness, recovery, sleep, activity, and wellness insights to the user through compatible applications.

The integration is not intended to sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it for commercial marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

The integration is designed to access data for the user’s requested purposes.

Whether information is stored, cached, or retained depends on the integration’s implementation, its configuration, and the applications used with it. Users should review the configuration of their MCP client and any connected services to understand their data-handling practices.

Personal information will not be intentionally sold or shared by the developer for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

This integration may interact with third-party services, including WHOOP and compatible MCP clients or AI applications.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. The developer does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

Users should review the relevant policies before authorizing access to their data.

6. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect authentication credentials and personal information. Access tokens, API credentials, and other secrets should not be included in public source code, public repositories, or publicly accessible configuration files.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. User Control and Permissions

Users control whether they authorize the integration to access their WHOOP account.

Users may revoke access through the relevant WHOOP account or authorization settings, where supported. Users may also stop using the integration at any time.

Revoking access may prevent the integration from retrieving additional information but may not automatically delete data already retained by connected third-party services.

8. Children’s Privacy

This integration is not specifically designed for children. It should only be used by individuals who are legally permitted to authorize access to the relevant account and data.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the integration’s functionality or data-handling practices change. Updates will be published in this repository with a revised effective date.
